**AI-Assisted Prediction and Analysis of Phase-Separated Morphology in Amphiphilic Polymer Conetworks**

ASUS UGen AI League 2026 | Stage I — Project Proposal

This document contains scientific references supporting the material background, phase separation mechanisms, machine learning methodology, and AI-assisted workflow proposed in this project.

## A. Amphiphilic Polymer Conetworks

**[1] G. Erdodi and J. P. Kennedy (2006)**

"Amphiphilic conetworks: Definition, synthesis, applications."

*Progress in Polymer Science*, vol. 31, no. 1, pp. 1–18, 2006.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.progpolymsci.2005.11.001

**Relevance:** Fundamental definition, synthesis, and applications of APCNs.

**[2] S. Ulrich, A. Sadeghpour, R. M. Rossi, N. Bruns, and L. F. Boesel (2018)**

"Wide Range of Functionalized Poly(N-alkyl acrylamide)-Based Amphiphilic Polymer Conetworks via Active Ester Precursors."

*Macromolecules*, vol. 51, no. 14, pp. 5267–5277, 2018.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.macromol.8b00841

**Relevance:** Synthesis of PDMS-containing APCNs, nanophase-separated structures, and functionalization of polymer networks.

**[3] C.-S. Huang et al. (2020)**

"Nano-domains assisted energy transfer in amphiphilic polymer conetworks for wearable luminescent solar concentrators."

*Nano Energy*, vol. 76, Art. no. 105039, 2020.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nanoen.2020.105039

**Relevance:** Demonstrates how hydrophilic and hydrophobic nanodomains in APCNs facilitate energy transfer between luminescent materials.

**[4] C.-S. Huang et al. (2022)**

## Research Scope and Limitations

The references provide scientific motivation and methodological foundations for the proposed AI-assisted APCN research framework.

Studies of viscoelastic phase separation in polymer solutions and colloidal systems provide relevant theoretical insights but do not independently establish viscoelastic phase separation behavior in the APCN system under investigation.

Likewise, previous machine learning studies demonstrate relevant computational approaches but do not establish the prediction accuracy of the project's GP-based model.

Project-specific simulation results and machine learning evaluations will be used to establish the capabilities and limitations of the proposed framework.
